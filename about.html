About SwiftShift Movers

A moving company built on one simple rule: the price confirmed is the price charged.

Why we started

SwiftShift Movers was started by two friends who had both been burned by bad movers: late trucks, mystery charges and a broken dining table that nobody owned up to. They decided the fix was boring but simple. Clear prices in writing before the move. Trained crews who treat a bookshelf like it matters. And a rule that the price confirmed is the price charged.

Years later the company is bigger, with more crews and a small storage facility, but the rules have not changed. We move homes and offices across the city and between cities, and we still walk every job with the customer at the start and the end.

Trained and checked crews

Every crew member is trained, uniformed and checked before they join a team. A crew leader runs every job and walks the home or office with you before and after the move.

How your items are protected

Furniture is wrapped, floors are protected, and every box is labelled by room. Fragile items are packed with bubble wrap in firm boxes, never loose in the truck.

If something goes wrong

If we damage something, we say so on the spot and the fix process starts the same day. Repairs first, and a fair settlement for anything beyond repair. Nothing is hidden.
