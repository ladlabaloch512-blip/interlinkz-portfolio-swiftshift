Moving Checklist

A simple week by week list, the same one our crew leaders give customers. The tick boxes are just for reading along; nothing here needs filling in.

4 weeks out

  • Book your move and lock the date with the deposit.
  • Walk through each room and decide what is not moving with you.
  • Start using up food from the fridge and freezer.
  • Tell your building management about the move, and ask about lift and parking rules.

1 week out

  • Confirm your final price in writing after the survey or call.
  • Pack the rooms you use least first, and label every box by room.
  • Keep documents, jewellery and medicines in a bag that travels with you, not in the truck.
  • Arrange a parking space for the truck at both addresses.

Moving day

  • Walk the home with the crew leader before loading starts.
  • Check every room, cupboard and balcony once loading is done.
  • If anything is damaged, note it with the crew leader on the day, on the job sheet.
  • Pay the balance only after the last box is in place.

First night

  • Open the kitchen and bedroom boxes first; the room labels make this quick.
  • Make the beds and find towels before you unpack anything else.
  • Plug in the fridge and let it settle before filling it.
  • If you spot any damage, tell us straight away so the fix process starts the same day.
