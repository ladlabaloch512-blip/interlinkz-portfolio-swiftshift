Home Moving

Your home moved by a crew sized to the job, with a clear from price before you book and the balance paid only after the last box is in.

What a home move includes

  • A trained crew sized to your home, from 3 crew for a studio to 6 for a large house
  • A crew leader who walks the home with you before loading and after unloading
  • Furniture wrapped and floors protected as standard
  • Every box labelled by room, so unpacking is not a treasure hunt
  • Transport, loading and unloading in one booking
  • Balance paid only when the last box is in place

Home moving prices
