Packing and Storage

Proper packing by the same crew that moves you, and clean, locked storage when your dates do not line up.

The packing service

Good packing is the difference between a move and a mess. Our crew wraps furniture, protects corners and screens, and packs boxes by room with clear labels. Fragile items get bubble wrap and a firm box, never a bag.

Materials we bring

Included with the packing service. Packing yourself? We can deliver the same bundle before your move; ask when you book.

Cardboard boxes

Strong double wall boxes in sizes for books, kitchen items and clothes.

Packing tape

Every box sealed properly, top and bottom, so nothing opens in the truck.

Bubble wrap

For screens, glass, mirrors and anything fragile.

Furniture blankets

Thick blankets wrap every furniture piece before it is carried out.

Room labels

Every box is labelled by room, so the right box lands in the right room.
