How the quote maths works

The instant estimate is simple, and it is the same maths on every page of this site:

  • Start with the base price for your home or office size.
  • Add the distance band: nothing within the city, Rs 15,000 for a nearby city, Rs 40,000 as the starting band for intercity.
  • Add any add ons you tick. The packing price depends on your size group, and office moves use the large home packing rate.
  • Storage is booked separately at per week and is never inside the estimate.

The result is shown as "Estimated from", because the final price is confirmed after a free survey or a short call. The confirmed price never goes up on moving day unless you add work.

Booking terms, in plain words

  • Booking deposit: to lock your date, taken off your final bill.
  • The rest is paid on moving day, after the last box is in.
  • Free date change up to 48 hours before the move.
  • No hidden charges: if it is not on this page or in your written confirmation, we do not charge it.
